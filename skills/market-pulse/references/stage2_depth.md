# Stage 2: Deep Observation (Depth, Logic & Proxies)

## Objective
Discard retail ("leek") narratives. Search for top-tier, in-depth, and timely institutional/contrarian research related to the "Biggest Thing" and emerging themes identified in Stage 1. Extract the true underlying logic and define falsifiable metrics.

## Execution Steps

1. **Contrarian & Deep Search:**
   - AI training data defaults to retail thinking. You must actively search for outlier, contrarian, and high-depth analyses to separate your findings from common retail noise.
   - Look for how *top-tier analysts* and institutional money are viewing the current events.

2. **Extract the Core Logic:**
   - Identify the causal frameworks and hidden logic behind the event.
   - What is the unique perspective that differs significantly from public consensus or common sense?
   - Why is the smart money acting the way it is?

3. **Identify the Core Proxy (核心观测指标):**
   - Narrative must be backed by data. Find the *Core Proxy*.
   - This must be a specific, observable fact, metric, or data point (e.g., a specific commodity price spread, a high-frequency supply chain metric, a specific policy wording).
   - This proxy must possess the "hardness" to either **confirm** or **falsify** the current narrative.

4. **Filter and Purify:**
   - Financial intelligence is about extracting truth from noise.
   - Filter out the weak arguments and distill the findings down to the "minority truth".

## Search Strategy (Depth)
- Query specifically for deep-dive reports, institutional perspectives, or contrarian takes on the themes found in Stage 1.
- E.g., `"[Theme] 深度 研报"`, `"[Theme] 核心矛盾"`, `"[Theme] 证伪"`.

## Output for Stage 2
Compile the core logic, the contrarian view, and the explicit Core Proxies. Again, use this internally or as an intermediate step to feed into Stage 3.
