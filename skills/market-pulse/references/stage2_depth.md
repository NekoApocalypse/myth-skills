# Stage 2: Deep Observation (Depth, Logic & Proxies)

## Objective
Discard retail ("leek") narratives. Search for top-tier, in-depth, and timely institutional/contrarian research related to the themes identified in Stage 1. Filter events using professional investment logic, extract underlying causal frameworks, and define falsifiable metrics.

## Execution Steps

1. **Contrarian & Deep Search:**
   - AI training data defaults to retail thinking. Actively search for outlier, contrarian, and high-depth analyses to separate your findings from common retail noise. Look for how *top-tier analysts* view the events.
   - **Reverse Attribution:** Price surges or news hype are surface-level. Identify the real event capital is using as an excuse.
   - **2nd-Order Effects:** Good opportunities often lie in second-order transmission chains. Search deeper if necessary.

2. **Filter via Investment Logic (投资逻辑):**
   Evaluate the raw events against these criteria:
   - **Capital Volume (潜在资金体量):** Does it affect massive industry capital (e.g., supply chain crisis) or support massive capital deployment (e.g., huge market size)?
   - **Rarity (稀有度):** Is it a "once-in-a-decade but happens every year" major opportunity?
   - **Foresight (超前性逻辑 - CRITICAL):**
     - *Early Stage:* High capital potential but no price movement yet.
     - *Hidden Stage:* Partially priced in but no broad trend.
     - *Trend Stage:* Priced in and trending. Watch for overheating or capital rotation.
     - *Golden Rule:* Facts precede expectations, expectations precede prices. The best buying point is when facts are set, expectations are forming, but capital hasn't fully arrived.
   - **Risk/Reward (综合逻辑):** A "perfect storm" is high certainty, early timing, and mild capital validation without overheating. If not perfect, prioritize high risk/reward ratios.

3. **Extract the Core Logic:**
   - Identify the causal frameworks and hidden logic. What is the unique perspective that differs from public consensus?

4. **Identify the Core Proxy (核心观测指标):**
   - Narrative must be backed by data. Find the *Core Proxy*.
   - This must be a specific, observable fact, metric, or data point (e.g., commodity price spread, supply chain metric, policy wording).
   - This proxy must possess the "hardness" to either **confirm** or **falsify** the narrative.

## Output for Stage 2
Compile the filtered events, core logic, contrarian views, and explicit Core Proxies. Use this internally to feed into Stage 3.
