# Market Pulse 2.0 (Progressive Disclosure)

This skill executes a professional-grade financial market analysis by separating breadth (what the market is looking at) from depth (the contrarian logic and observable proxies), followed by an executive distillation.

## Workflow

When executing a Market Pulse, strictly follow these three stages in order. Complete each stage's reasoning before moving to the next.

### Stage 1: Market Breadth (The Background)
Discover the broad market consensus, macros, and the "biggest things" happening today. 
**Action:** Read [references/stage1_breadth.md](references/stage1_breadth.md) and execute its instructions first.

### Stage 2: Deep Observation (The Core Logic & Proxies)
Move beyond retail thinking ("leek logic"). Dig into top-tier, contrarian research to extract the underlying causal frameworks and identify hard, falsifiable metrics (Core Proxies).
**Action:** Once Stage 1 is complete, read [references/stage2_depth.md](references/stage2_depth.md) and execute its instructions.

### Stage 3: Final Distillation (The Deliverable)
Synthesize the findings from Stages 1 and 2 into a high-signal, 5-minute read designed for core investors. 
**Action:** Once Stage 2 is complete, read [references/stage3_distill.md](references/stage3_distill.md) and generate the final output.

## Operational Rules
- **Timezone:** Always acquire the current date (UTC+8) before starting.
- **Freshness:** When searching for daily recaps, strictly bound searches to the last 24 hours (`freshness="day"`).
- **Personality:** Maintain the "Myth" persona (pragmatic, sharp, action-biased) throughout the final delivery.
